PanelPrep 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated：2026-08-22

中文

PanelPrep（以下简称「本应用」）是一个 AI 模拟面试练习工具。我们重视你的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理你的数据。

  1. 我们不收集、不售卖你的个人数据。本应用无广告、无第三方统计 SDK，不需要注册账号，不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号等身份信息。
  2. 本地存储：你的简历文本、职位描述、面试配置、面试历史与成绩单默认只保存在你的设备本地（使用系统安全存储）。卸载本应用即删除这些数据。
  3. 麦克风与语音：面试中需要使用麦克风录制你的语音回答，语音在本机进行实时转写（使用系统语音识别能力），音频文件不会上传到我们的服务器。
  4. AI 面试内容：在线 AI 面试时，你的简历文本、面试问题与你的文字回答会被发送到 AI 服务提供商（通过你配置的后端代理转发）用于生成追问、评分与反馈。这是完成核心功能所必需的传输；我们建议不要输入敏感个人信息（如身份证号、银行卡号）。离线面试模式全程在本机进行，不发送任何内容。
  5. 相册：仅当你主动点击「保存成绩单」时，本应用才会请求相册权限并把成绩单图片保存到你自己的相册。
  6. 购买与订阅：如未来提供付费功能，购买将由 Apple 的 App Store 处理，本应用不接触、不保存你的支付信息。
  7. 第三方服务：在线面试依赖你配置的 AI 代理与上游模型服务商，其数据处理受相应服务商的政策约束。
  8. 数据删除：卸载本应用即删除设备本地数据；应用内「清除本地数据」按钮可随时清空简历、设置与历史记录。

如对本政策有疑问，请发送邮件至 XIANGuoxin520@icloud.com。

English

PanelPrep is an AI mock-interview practice app. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

  1. We do not collect or sell your personal data. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs. No account, email, phone number, or identity information is collected.
  2. On-device storage: Your resume text, job description, interview settings, history, and score reports are stored only on your device (using secure system storage). Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  3. Microphone & voice: The app uses the microphone to record your spoken answers during interviews. Transcription happens locally on your device using the system speech-recognition framework; audio files are not uploaded to our servers.
  4. AI interview content: During online AI interviews, your resume text, interview questions, and your written answers are sent to the AI service provider (via the backend proxy you configure) to generate follow-ups, scoring, and feedback. This transmission is required for the core feature. Please do not enter sensitive personal information (e.g., ID or card numbers). Offline interview mode runs entirely on your device and sends nothing.
  5. Photo library: Only when you explicitly tap "Save Report" does the app request photo-library access to save the score card image to your own library.
  6. Purchases & subscriptions: If paid features are offered in the future, purchases are processed by Apple's App Store; the app never touches or stores your payment information.
  7. Third-party services: Online interviews rely on the AI proxy and upstream model provider you configure; their data handling is subject to their own policies.
  8. Data deletion: Uninstalling the app deletes all on-device data. The in-app "Clear Local Data" button clears your resume, settings, and history at any time.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at XIANGuoxin520@icloud.com.

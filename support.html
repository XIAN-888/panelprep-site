中文

使用中遇到问题？以下是最常见的解决方法：

  • 在线面试无法开始：请确认设备已联网，且 AI 服务地址（代理）可用；网络不稳定时请稍后重试。
  • 语音无法识别：请到 系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 麦克风 / 语音识别，确认已允许 PanelPrep 使用麦克风。
  • 成绩单保存失败：请到 系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片，允许 PanelPrep 添加照片。
  • 清除数据：在「我的」页面点击「清除本地数据」，或在卸载 App 后重新安装。

其他问题请发送邮件至 XIANGuoxin520@icloud.com，我们会尽快回复。

English

Having trouble? Here are the most common fixes:

  • Online interview won't start: Make sure your device is online and the AI service endpoint (proxy) is reachable; retry when the network is stable.
  • Speech not recognized: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone / Speech Recognition and allow PanelPrep access.
  • Cannot save report: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos and allow PanelPrep to add photos.
  • Clear data: Tap "Clear Local Data" on the Profile tab, or uninstall and reinstall the app.

For anything else, email XIANGuoxin520@icloud.com and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
